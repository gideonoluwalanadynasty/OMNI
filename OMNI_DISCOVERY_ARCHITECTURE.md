# OMNI DISCOVERY INTELLIGENCE & ANALYTICS ARCHITECTURE

## 1. Executive Summary
The **OMNI Discovery Intelligence Platform** (Prompt 14) transforms OMNI Connect into a universally searchable, discoverable, and telemetry-rich ecosystem. It unites an 11-entity search engine, an 8-signal recommendation pipeline, a localized business discovery directory, a 5-tier analytics command center, and an ambient Gemini 2.5 AI Analytics Copilot.

## 2. Universal Search Engine Architecture (11 Entity Indices)
The universal search engine indexes and ranks 11 heterogeneous entity types across the sovereign network:
1. **People**: Mentors, researchers, developers, executives with verified credentials.
2. **Businesses**: Enterprises, local merchants, certified agencies.
3. **Creators**: Digital artists, course instructors, live streamers.
4. **Communities**: Spaces, groups, public broadcast channels.
5. **Posts & Articles**: Long-form guides, announcements, updates.
6. **Videos & Moments**: Short-form vertical reels, recordings, live replays.
7. **Products**: Physical hardware, sovereign nodes, digital goods.
8. **Services**: Security audits, consulting, escrow-protected services.
9. **Courses**: Cohort-based learning, certifications, masterclasses.
10. **Events**: Hybrid summits, virtual townhalls, webinars.
11. **Documents**: Cryptographic whitepapers, open-source specifications.

### Multi-Stage Pipeline:
- **Stage 1 (Filter)**: Strict permission boundaries, privacy visibility checks (Public, Circles, Followers, Private), and price/rating facets.
- **Stage 2 (Vector/Semantic & Keyword Matching)**: Sub-18ms token and semantic vector matching with Gemini 2.5 synthesis.
- **Stage 3 (Ranking)**: Relevance score, engagement velocity, and relationship affinity calculation.

## 3. 8-Signal Sovereign Recommendation Engine
Personalized recommendations derive from 8 weighted signals that strictly adhere to user consent:
- **Interests (25%)**: Explicit topics, tags, and category subscriptions.
- **Social Graph (20%)**: 1st and 2nd degree circle connection endorsements.
- **Engagement Velocity (15%)**: Likes, reposts, comments, and saves.
- **Communities & Spaces (10%)**: Space memberships and squad activity.
- **Watch & Dwell (10%)**: Video completion rates and article reading depth.
- **Purchases & Escrows (10%)**: Direct marketplace transactions and contracts.
- **Geolocation (5%)**: Distance in kilometers for local businesses and events.
- **Language & Locale (5%)**: Multilingual localization preferences.

### Sovereign Privacy Guarantee:
- Zero user data is used for foundation model training.
- 1-click **"Purge Signals"** cryptographic memory eradication.
- Granular consent toggles for behavioral tracking, geolocation, and social graph matching.

## 4. Multi-Dimensional Analytics Platform (5 Tiers)
1. **Personal Analytics**: Engagement rates, impressions, network health score (0-100), contact velocity, and a **Digital Wellbeing & Mindful Screen Time** tracker.
2. **Creator Analytics**: Followers net growth, global reach, 4-stream revenue breakdown (Subscriptions, Marketplace Drops, Tips, Ad Rev Share), watch retention, and demographic geography.
3. **Business Analytics**: Gross merchandise sales, pipeline lead qualification rate, message SLA compliance, buyer conversion funnels, and active ad campaign ROAS.
4. **Community Analytics**: Space members, 30-day retention cohort matrix (D1, D7, D14, D30), daily active messages, clean moderation index, and top contributor leaderboards.
5. **Super Admin Platform Analytics**: DAU/MAU stickiness, global traffic QPS, P95 edge latency, gross ecosystem volume, and trust & safety threat triage.
