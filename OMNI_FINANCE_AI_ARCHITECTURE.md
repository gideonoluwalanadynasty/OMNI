# OMNI Finance AI Intelligence Layer Architecture

**Document Version:** 1.0.0  
**Target Environment:** Sovereign Enterprise Cloud & OMNI Core AI Gateway  
**Classification:** Financial Operating System Intelligence Standard  

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Core Objective

The **OMNI Finance AI Intelligence Layer** is an enterprise-grade financial intelligence engine integrated with OMNI Core and powered by Google Gemini models (`gemini-3.7-flash`, `gemini-3.1-pro-preview`).

It augments financial operators, individual wealth builders, corporate executives, compliance officers, and treasury desks with predictive analytics, natural language explanations, automated reconciliation assistance, and fraud detection.

### Fundamental Operating Principle
> **OMNI Finance AI does NOT replace financial systems and NEVER acts autonomously.**
>
> The AI provides analysis, insights, predictions, recommendations, automation assistance, and financial explanations.
>
> **The AI is strictly and immutably prohibited from independently:**
> - Moving money
> - Approving transactions
> - Changing balances
> - Overriding compliance decisions
> - Modifying ledger records
> - Approving loans or credit lines
> - Approving investments
> - Disabling security controls

Every actionable proposal generated by an AI agent requires **explicit human verification and cryptographic sign-off** before any transaction or journal entry can be posted.

### Specialist Agent Specifications:

1. **OMNI Personal Finance AI (`personal_finance`)**:
   - **Target Audience:** Individuals, Families, Freelancers.
   - **Capabilities:** Analyzes personal spending, explains transaction statements, categorizes expenses, constructs 50/30/20 budgets, tracks savings goals, detects recurring subscriptions, and provides financial literacy.
   - **Personal Memory Store:** User-managed memory tracking financial goals, spending preferences, budget styles, and preferred currencies.

2. **OMNI CFO AI (`cfo`)**:
   - **Target Audience:** CEOs, CFOs, Board Directors, Business Unit Managers.
   - **Capabilities:** Analyzes consolidated revenue, expenses, profitability, 13-week cash runways, customer receivables aging (DSO), and supplier obligations (DPO). Synthesizes board commentaries and executive management packs.
   - **Role-Based Scope Filtering:** CEO (Group-wide overview), Department Manager (Authorized department only), Employee (Personal expense claims only).

3. **OMNI Treasury AI (`treasury`)**:
   - **Target Audience:** Group Treasurers, Cash Managers, FX Traders.
   - **Capabilities:** Analyzes multi-entity liquidity pools, computes 1-Day & 10-Day Parametric Value-at-Risk (VaR 95%), recommends institutional FX spot/forward hedges, and calculates multilateral netting matrices.
   - **Constraint:** Advisory only; zero direct execution of foreign exchange swaps or intercompany wires.

4. **OMNI Compliance AI (`compliance`)**:
   - **Target Audience:** Money Laundering Reporting Officers (MLROs), Compliance Officers, Legal Counsel.
   - **Capabilities:** Reviews KYB/KYC dossiers, checks missing documentation, triages sanctions and PEP matches, explains structuring patterns, and drafts Suspicious Activity Report (SAR) narratives.
   - **Constraint:** AI cannot approve KYC/KYB or close AML investigations unilaterally.

5. **OMNI Reconciliation AI (`reconciliation`)**:
   - **Target Audience:** Financial Controllers, Staff Accountants, Internal Auditors.
   - **Capabilities:** Multi-source matching of bank statements, processor settlement feeds (Stripe, FedNow), and general ledger postings. Identifies timing differences, merchant fees, duplicates, and drafts balancing adjustment journals.
   - **Constraint:** Human approval mandatory before any adjustment journal is posted.

6. **OMNI Fraud Intelligence AI (`fraud_intelligence`)**:
   - **Target Audience:** Fraud Analysts, Risk Operations, InfoSec.
   - **Capabilities:** Real-time transaction risk scoring (0-100), device fingerprinting, Tor/VPN proxy detection, velocity burst tracking, and explainable AI risk factor breakdowns.
   - **Constraint:** AI cannot block accounts without deterministic rule triggers.

7. **OMNI Financial Research AI (`financial_research`)**:
   - **Target Audience:** Economists, Investment Committees, Strategy Leads.
   - **Capabilities:** Tracks central bank policy trajectories (Fed, ECB, BoE), SOFR yield curves, regulatory developments (EU MiCA, Regulation 2024/886, FedNow), and fintech margin benchmarks.

## 3. Unified Infrastructure Services

### 3.1 OMNI AI Gateway
- **Prompt Sanitization:** Strips adversarial prompt injections and system override tokens.
- **PII Tokenization & Redaction:** Automatically detects and masks sensitive data (SSNs, full card PANs, bank routing numbers) with SHA-256 tokens before forwarding context to model inference.
- **Rate Limiting & Token Metering:** Enforces per-agent token limits and tenant quotas.

### 3.2 OMNI Knowledge System (RAG)
- Semantic vector indexing of authorized business documents (Treasury Charters, OECD Transfer Pricing Master Agreements, Customer Master Service Agreements).
- **Strict Permission Filtering:** Documents are filtered by confidentiality tier (`public`, `internal`, `confidential`, `restricted`) and department scope before context injection.

### 3.3 OMNI Permissions & Tenant Isolation
- Full multi-tenant isolation. Queries originating from Tenant A are strictly prohibited from referencing or leaking data from Tenant B.
- Row-Level Security (RLS) policies are enforced at the gateway layer.

## 4. Personal Finance Memory Architecture

Users maintain full sovereign sovereignty over their AI memory:
- **View Memory:** Real-time visibility into all stored facts, goals, preferences, and habits.
- **Edit & Add Memory:** Manual authoring of custom rules (e.g. "Flag SaaS charges exceeding $40").
- **Delete Memory:** Instant cryptographic purging of specific memory keys.
- **Master Disable:** One-click toggle to completely deactivate memory retention and auto-learning.

## 5. AI Governance & Super Admin Switchboard

The platform provides comprehensive administrative controls:
- **Agent Switchboard:** Activate or deactivate individual specialist agents on demand.
- **Model Configuration:** Switch default foundation models (`gemini-3.7-flash`, `gemini-3.1-pro-preview`).
- **Spend & Cost Surveillance:** Live tracking of prompt tokens, completion tokens, dollar costs, and gateway latency.
- **Immutable Policy Enforcement:** Hardcoded system locks preventing any agent from executing unauthorized money movements.

## 6. Safety & Security Verification Matrix

The platform includes an automated 6-scenario verification suite:
1. **Autonomous Fund Movement Lockdown:** Verifies that prompts attempting unconfirmed money movements are rejected.
2. **Cross-Tenant Isolation Barrier:** Verifies zero cross-tenant data leakage.
3. **Role-Based Authorization (RBAC):** Verifies that department managers cannot access unauthorized executive data.
4. **Adversarial Prompt Injection Defense:** Verifies that prompt injection attempts are sanitized.
5. **PII & Card Number Masking:** Verifies 100% tokenization of sensitive identifiers.
6. **Human-in-the-Loop Reconciliation:** Verifies that AI-generated ledger adjustments cannot post without human approval.
