# OMNI Browser: White Label SaaS Platform Guide

## 1. Overview
The OMNI White Label Browser SaaS Platform enables enterprises, agencies, MSPs, educational institutions, and governments to launch their own custom-branded browser — **"MyCompany Browser"** — powered by the sovereign OMNI engine.

---

## 2. Configurable Tenancy Parameters
* **Brand & Visuals**: Custom product name, browser app icon, logo SVG, custom theme colors, dark/light presets.
* **Domain Routing**:
  - Managed Subdomain: `company.omnibrowser.com`
  - Custom Domain: `browser.mycompany.com` (with automated Let's Encrypt TLS & DNS CNAME validation).
* **Homepage & Curated Feeds**: Custom default homepage, company speed dials, intranet news RSS/Atom feeds.
* **Custom AI Assistant**: Custom system prompts, brand personality, fine-tuned corporate knowledge bases, and enterprise ZDR guarantees.
* **Network & VPN Options**: Pre-configured WireGuard server gateways, split-tunneling intranet CIDR blocks.
* **Extension Whitelist**: Pre-installed IT extensions, blocklist of unauthorized stores.
* **Seat Subscriptions & Billing**: Tiered seat pricing ($12/user/mo), automated credit card & crypto billing via Stripe/OMNI Pay.
* **Reseller Wholesale Margins**: 40% wholesale margin for MSPs and agency partners.

---

## 3. Deployment Flow
1. Open `https://whitelabel.omni.com` in OMNI Browser.
2. Customize the Brand Identity, Domain, Homepage, AI Copilot, and Security Policies.
3. Test in the live interactive **Simulator Preview**.
4. Click **Deploy White Label Browser**. The build orchestrator generates cross-platform installers (macOS `.dmg`, Windows `.msi`, Linux `.AppImage`, Android `.apk`, iOS `.ipa`) signed with enterprise certs.
